AI Limitations & Truth Boundaries

  • Zero Hallucinated Numbers: The Groq LLM receives pre-calculated numerical facts and is strictly instructed never to fabricate calculations.
  • Correlation ≠ Causation: Statistical associations observed across dimensions do not necessarily imply causal relationships.
  • Scenario Estimates: What-if calculations are based on user assumptions and mathematical sensitivities, not speculative fortune-telling.
  • Explicit Insufficient Data Safeguard: When asked queries without supporting dimensions (e.g., dropout risk without historical targets), the system explicitly refuses with an Insufficient Data notice.

Data Health Score Breakdown

The Data Health Score is calculated deterministically via defined rules without arbitrary LLM guessing:

Score = (Completeness × 0.30) + (Consistency × 0.25) + (Validity × 0.25) + (Duplicates × 0.20)
Completeness (Non-null ratio): 92%
Consistency (Uniform types): 88%
Validity (Valid boundaries): 90%
Duplicates (Uniqueness): 98%
